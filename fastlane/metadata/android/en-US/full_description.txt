Noctune is an open source music player for Android with two modes: your own local music and online streaming.

<b>Local mode</b>
• Plays the music stored on your phone.
• Search your library with suggestions from your previous searches and your local songs.

<b>Streaming mode</b>
• Search and stream music from Audius, the decentralized music platform.
• Search and play free audio from Internet Archive: public domain recordings, live concerts, netlabels, audiobooks and old time radio. The license of each item is shown when the uploader declares one.

<b>More</b>
• Playlists, favorites and listening history.
• Synced lyrics from LrcLib.
• Sleep timer, offline downloads and a guest mode.

Noctune has no ads and no tracking, and it does not use Google services.
